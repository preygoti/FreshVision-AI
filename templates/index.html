How Grad-CAM works

Grad-CAM uses gradients from the model to highlight image regions that contributed to a prediction. It provides a visual explanation of the model's attention, but does not prove that the prediction is correct.

Get Better Results

Simple guidelines for optimal visual classification:

  • ✓ Use a clear image
  • ✓ Keep the fruit clearly visible
  • ✓ Use good lighting
  • ✓ Avoid heavy shadows
  • ✓ Avoid excessive background clutter
  • ✓ Prefer one main fruit in the image
Transparency

Know the Limits

  • Scope: The current model supports three fruit types and six freshness categories.
  • Image Quality: Lighting, camera angle, image quality, and background conditions can affect predictions.
  • Visual Classification: The system evaluates visible image patterns and cannot determine internal fruit condition or microscopic properties.
  • Unsupported Images: Images outside the supported categories may produce unreliable predictions.
  • Academic Prototype: FreshVision AI is an academic AI prototype and is not a certified food-safety assessment.

About FreshVision AI

FreshVision AI is an academic deep-learning project that explores computer vision for visual fruit freshness classification. The system combines transfer learning with MobileNetV2 and Grad-CAM to provide both predictions and visual explanations.
